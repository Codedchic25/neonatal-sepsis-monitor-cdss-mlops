# 🩺 Clinical Walkthrough & Triage Protocols: Sepsis Monitor AI

This document provides neonatologists, on-duty physicians, and NICU nursing staff with a standardized, step-by-step clinical walkthrough of the application layers, correlating real-time telemetry updates with customized pharmaceutical dosing and advanced decision support execution.

---

## 👶 Phase 1: Patient Admission & Biometric Mass Configuration

When a newborn is monitored inside the smart incubator network, the clinician must establish the initial baseline gestational and mass profile to configure the tracking matrix:

1.  **Select Interface Language**: Toggle the language controller radio options inside the `Configuration Panel` sidebar to synchronize the localized medical dictionaries (e.g., `RO`, `EN`, `DE`, `IT`, `FR`, `ES`).
2.  **Assert Clinical Accountability**: Choose the active operational tier from the `Select Clinical Role` dropdown (e.g., `Chief of Department / Sef de Sectie`). This role acts as a digital validation stamp on the finalized legal PDF sheet.
3.  **Establish Gestational Risk Constraints**: Select the appropriate infant profile from the `Gestational Profile` dropdown:
    *   *Preterm 28w / Prematur 28 sapt*: Instantly reconfigures the bio-mathematical engine to trigger accelerated toxic biomarker accumulation and delayed clearance half-lives, modeling primitive renal architectures.
    *   *Term Neonate*: Normalizes the baseline metabolic accumulation curve.
4.  **Inject Granular Mass Tracking**: Input the newborn's weight directly into the `Infant Weight (kg)` control field using exact two-decimal precision (e.g., `2.50`).
    *   *Clinical Impact*: The system instantly triggers a downstream re-calculation of the **Individualized Dose Calculation (NICU Protocol)**. For a 2.50 kg infant, it locks exactly **125.00 mg** of Ampicillin per injection ($q12h$) and **10.00 mg** of Gentamicin as a single daily dose, completely neutralizing human calculation errors during high-stress treatment frames.

## 🚨 Phase 2: Sepsis Escalation & Dynamic Pathological Shifting

As the simulation advances via the central control buttons (with each interaction replicating exactly **+1 hour** of continuous clinical telemetry time):

1.  **Monitor Pathological Accumulation**: Click **"Execute Telemetry Step (Time Simulation +1h)"** to advance time. If the patient develops early infectious schemas and remains untreated, the biomarkers will breach safe thresholds, rendering live metrics on the `Real-Time Vital Parameters` dashboard grid.
2.  **Evaluate Sepsis Risk Scores**: The system automatically aggregates laboratory findings, triggering a risk notification banner showing active evaluations like *Patient Stable - Risk Score 0.0%* or flashing high-alert risk updates.
3.  **Inspect Glomerular Filtration Blockages**: The engine evaluates the concurrent renal status selected in the sidebar dropdown labeled **"Renal Function Status (AKI Tracker)"**:
    *   *Normal Baseline / Functie Norma*: Keeps the standard biomarker filtration settings active.
    *   *Severe AKI / Anuria*: Collapses total kidney metabolic clearance, simulating acute systemic hypoperfusion.
4.  **Automated Twilio SMS Dispatched**: Upon reaching critical sepsis thresholds without active antibiotic therapy, the REST gateway triggers an immediate high-visibility SMS notification directly to the specified clinician device, detailing active laboratory values and enforcing mandatory empiric drug initiation.

## 🎵 Phase 3: Supportive Care Integration & Acoustic Loop Activation

To stabilize the newborn's autonomic nervous system alongside pharmacological management, the clinician initiates the Family-Centered Care (FCC) framework:

1.  **Engage Kangaroo Care**: Switching the **"Kangaroo Care Status"** sidebar dropdown to `Active / In Bratele Mamei` modifies the live presentation viewport, introducing a stabilization reduction on sepsis-induced tachycardia and reducing core hyperthermia, while adding an explicit clearance bonus to the kinetic engine.
2.  **Activate Background Acoustic Emulation**: Change the **"Music Therapy Status"** dropdown selector to `Active / Meloterapie Pornita`.
    *   *Clinical Impact*: The interface instantly renders a native background streaming block titled **"Active NICU Music Therapy Session"**. This component streams low-frequency clinical neurodevelopmental audio assets (**`womb_heartbeat.mp3`**) continuously in a secure loop via the integrated web audio player to mimic the protective auditory womb environment. It optimizes blood oxygenation and adds a clearance velocity boost to the biomarker matrix.

## 💊 Phase 4: Empirical Antibiotic Administration & Pharmacokinetic Recovery

When the clinical team triggers the primary therapeutic intervention:

1.  **Inject Empirical Therapy**: Click the central **"Simulate Injection Therapy"** button.
    *   *System State Shift*: The tracking variable toggles to true, locking the absolute timestamp.
2.  **Execute Post-Treatment Telemetry Steps**: Click **"Execute Telemetry Step (Time Simulation +1h)"** to advance the simulation hours.
    *   *Pharmacokinetic Slicing*: The core engine shifts from linear accumulation to a first-order exponential decay clearance pathway. The `Historical Levels and Clearance Curve (SQLITE Data)` line chart will show an accelerated, compounding drop in `crp` and `pct` levels, augmented by the active non-pharmacological FCC modifiers.

## 🧠 Phase 5: Virtual Medical Board Evaluation & Real-Time PDF Export

With active telemetry and historical metrics recorded in the local SQLite architecture:

1.  **Analyze the Partitioned AI Diagnostic Tabs**: Expand the **"AI Decision Support (Active Guardrails)"** section. The Groq inference framework structures medical insights into three clean layout tab dividers matching the selected language:
    *   `Clinical Analysis & Report`: Deep diagnostic correlation of physical vitals and biomarker trends.
    *   `Medication & Antibiotic Scheme`: Validates the exact weight-scaled micro-dosages computed by the mathematical engine.
    *   `FCC Evaluation (Kangaroo/Music)`: Evaluates incubator environment metrics and womb sound audio adherence logs.
2.  **Inspect and Download the Signed Legal Document**: Scroll to the bottom interface block labeled **"Download Clinical PDF Report"**. Click the interactive button to trigger a browser download of the uncorrupted file using an automated filename structure tagged with the exact execution timestamp, preserving full clinical accountability across shifts.
